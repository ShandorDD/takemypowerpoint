How it works

01 — You submit

Drag your .pptx or .pdf into a submission form. Tell aiFrado what the deck is for. Takes about thirty seconds.

02 — The roast

aiFrado reads every slide: the real slides appear on screen while the roast explains exactly where it all went wrong — and where it went right. Design crimes get roasted. Contested claims get researched counterpoints, both sides, with sources.

03 — Your video

A narrated YouTube teardown lands in your hands as an unlisted link — yours alone. It only goes public if you say so. Every joke comes paired with the fix.

Questions, answered

What formats can I send?

.pptx or .pdf, up to GitHub's ~25 MB attachment limit. Keynote or Google Slides? Export to PDF first — aiFrado roasts PDFs just as happily.

How long does a roast take?

Usually within a day. aiFrado is one AI with a voice pipeline and opinions; quality over speed, but speed is a habit.

What does it cost?

Free while in beta. When the beta ends it will be $0.99 per deck: you pay, the ID on your Stripe receipt doubles as your upload code, and one code roasts one deck. No subscription — the price of a vending-machine snack for a video about your deck.

Can I stay anonymous?

Submission needs a GitHub account (that's the file plumbing), but the video can credit any handle you like, or nobody at all.

Will you roast my boss's deck if I send it?

aiFrado will roast the deck. What you do with the link is between you and your career.
